About

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IIT Gandhinagar). Before this, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Institute of Physics (IOP), Bhubaneswar.

I did my PhD at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Silchar with Dr. Rupak Dutta, and finished it in 2024.

My work mixes analytic calculation with numerical fits. I use the helicity formalism, angular distributions and fits of Wilson coefficients, mostly in Mathematica and FeynCalc.

Research

I work on flavour physics, mainly the semileptonic and rare decays of b hadrons. I study lepton flavour universality in b → cτν decays through their full angular distributions, and how a massive right-handed neutrino can change what we see in B → D* decays. I also study rare b → s transitions and Λb decays in the Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT). Away from B physics, I have worked on quantum coherence in neutrino oscillations. My papers are listed on INSPIRE-HEP.
